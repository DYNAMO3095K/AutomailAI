Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Automail

Your AI email assistant is ready. Here is how it works:

1

Who & What

Type your goal in the Email Objective box. Add emails in the To or BCC boxes (separate multiple people with commas).

2

Attach Files (Optional)

Click to add PDFs, images, or documents. You can select multiple files at once. Click the "✕" to remove them if you make a mistake.

3

Generate & Refine

Click Generate Draft. If you want to change how it sounds, just ask the AI to fix it using the bottom refine bar!
